# Shio support

Questions, bugs, or feedback: email **goutham2iosdev@gmail.com**. Please include your device and OS version.

## Common questions

**Why does Shio need my sleep data?**
Shio reads the sleep stages your Apple Watch, phone, or sleep tracker already recorded (Apple Health on iPhone, Health Connect on Android) to learn your own cycle length and score your nights. Access is read-only, and the data never leaves your device.

**My alarm didn't use the sound I picked.**
On iPhone, custom alarm sounds need iOS 26.1 or later; on iOS 26.0 alarms use the system tone. On Android, check that Shio is allowed to schedule exact alarms (Settings › Apps › Shio › Alarms & reminders).

**The dashboard is empty.**
Shio needs at least one night of recorded sleep stages. Check that your watch or tracker writes sleep to Apple Health or Health Connect, and that Shio has permission to read it.

**How do I cancel Shio+?**
iPhone: Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Android: Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Shio+ stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

**Restore a purchase on a new device.**
In Shio, open Settings, tap Shio+, then Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account.
